Choosing Your Hive Type

🎬
Video lesson content · 12 min

Selecting the right hive type is one of the most important early decisions a beekeeper makes. In South Australia, the Langstroth hive is the most commonly used due to its modular design and compatibility with standard equipment. This lesson walks through the pros and cons of the Langstroth, Warre, and Top Bar hive styles, with guidance on which best suits a beginner in the Adelaide climate.
